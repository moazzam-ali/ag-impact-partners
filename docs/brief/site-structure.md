Motion system

## Le fil: one line from first contact to signature.

A single thin line ties the whole site together. It is born in the homepage hero, where two lines from opposite edges meet like two sides of a table. It runs down every page, bends into each section’s key moment (the table that tips over, the cost bar that shrinks, the steps of a mandate) and ends every page as a signature stroke above the contact details. It tells the firm’s story without a word: they negotiate, and they carry it through to signature.

**One moment per section**

Each section has a single motion idea. Nothing else moves.

**Motion explains**

It shows a figure, a change of side, a sequence. Never decoration, never gradients or blurred shapes.

**Slow and precise**

Long, calm easing. Nothing bounces. It should feel like a senior firm, not a launch video.

**Readable without it**

Every section works as a still page. Reduced-motion settings get the static version.

**Fast**

SVG, CSS and GSAP ScrollTrigger only. No video, no heavy 3D. The client will check load times.

**Simpler on mobile**

Same story, shorter pinned sequences, thumb-friendly contact actions.

Copy rules

## From the brief, for every line.

**Facts before adjectives**

“Plus d’un million d’abonnés en moins d’un mois” beats “un succès retentissant”.

**Figures exactly as given**

No new figures, no rounding, no stronger labels. No founding date in any form.

**Their language**

Industry terms used without explanation. “Vous”, never “tu”. No superlatives, emoji or exclamation marks.

**The generic test**

If a sentence could sit on any consultancy’s site, rewrite it.

Banned phrases

partenaire de confiance · acteur incontournable · expertise unique · solution clé en main · équipe passionnée et engagée · solutions innovantes et sur mesure · au cœur de · à l’ère du digital · accompagnement sur mesure · catalogue de prestations
